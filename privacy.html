Privacy Policy

Hedwig Clips (the "Bot") · Effective 19 October 2026

What the Bot does

When a moderator or the broadcaster types !clip in a Kick channel's chat, the Bot asks Kick to save a short clip of the stream and posts a link to that clip as a message in a Discord channel chosen by the channel owner.

Information the Bot handles

  • Kick chat messages of the configured channel, which are publicly visible: username, message text and role badges. They are read only to detect the !clip command and who sent it.
  • Clip details returned by Kick: title, link, thumbnail and the name of the person who requested it. These are posted to Discord.
  • Discord identifiers such as the server and channel the owner configured, needed to post messages.

The Bot does not read Discord messages, does not collect Discord user profiles, and does not use cookies or analytics.

Storage and retention

The Bot does not keep a database. Chat messages are held in memory only long enough to check for the command. Short operational logs on the hosting service may briefly include the username of a person who issued a command or an error message. Clips themselves are stored by Kick, and posted messages are stored by Discord, under their own policies.

Sharing

We do not sell or share information. Data passes only through the services the Bot depends on: Kick, Discord and the hosting provider that runs it.

Your choices

Clips can be deleted on Kick by the channel owner, and the Discord message can be deleted by server moderators. To stop the Bot, remove it from your Discord server or stop using the !clip command. To ask about your data or request removal of a clip post, contact the operator below.

Children

The Bot is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age set by Discord and Kick in your country).

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with an updated date.
